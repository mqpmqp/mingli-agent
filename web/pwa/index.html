What these words mean / 术语说明

BaZi means “eight characters”: two characters for each of the year, month, day and hour pillars. “Day Master” names the first character of the day pillar in this tradition. These are chart labels, not measured personality traits. / 八字是年、月、日、时四柱的八个字；日主是日柱的天干。这些是传统排盘术语，不是经测量的人格特征。

Personalized interpretation is not available in this draft. The result uses the fields you entered; missing or uncertain time cannot be inferred. / 当前预览未提供个性化解读；结果依据你的输入，不能反推缺失或不确定的出生时间。

Privacy & method / 隐私与方法

Your birth details and results stay in this page’s memory. Reloading or clearing removes them. Only you can copy or download a result. No analytics, account or remote AI call is included. / 出生资料与结果仅在当前页面内存中，刷新或清空后消失；只有主动复制、下载才导出。无埋点、账号或远程 AI 调用。

The first visit downloads approximately 14 MB of static runtime files. Wait for “Offline ready” before disconnecting. Browsers may remove cached files. The static host may log IP and browser details; chart inputs are not sent. / 首次访问需下载约 14 MB 静态运行资源，显示离线就绪后再断网；浏览器可能清理缓存。静态托管方可能记录 IP 和浏览器信息，排盘输入不会上传。

What if my birth time is unknown? / 出生时间不详怎么办？

A precise chart needs a confirmed time; the form never substitutes midnight. Calendar boundaries may require review. / 精确排盘需要确认时间，不会默认为子时；历法边界可能需要复核。
